FCL vs LCL: Quick Comparison

FCL (Full Container Load)

You rent an entire container exclusively for your cargo.

  • Best for: 15+ CBM of cargo
  • Cost: Fixed per container
  • Transit: Faster (no consolidation)
  • Security: Sealed from origin

LCL (Less than Container Load)

Your cargo shares a container with other shippers.

  • Best for: Under 15 CBM
  • Cost: Pay per CBM used
  • Transit: Slower (3-7 days extra)
  • Security: More handling
