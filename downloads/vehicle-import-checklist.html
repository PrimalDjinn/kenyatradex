Vehicle Import to Kenya Checklist

Updated April 2026 | Complete Step-by-Step Guide

Important Reminders:
• Only Right-Hand Drive (RHD) vehicles are allowed
• Maximum age: 8 years from year of manufacture
• QISJ inspection certificate is mandatory
• Kenya Tradex handles the entire process for you
Step-by-Step Timeline

Week 1-2: Purchase & Inspection

Purchase vehicle, arrange QISJ pre-shipment inspection

Week 2-3: Shipping Arrangement

Book shipping (RoRo or container), prepare documentation

Week 3-8: Transit Time

Japan: 20-30 days | UAE: 7-12 days | UK: 20-30 days | South Africa: 7-14 days

Week 8-9: Customs Clearance

Mombasa port clearance: 3-7 days with complete documentation

Week 9-10: Delivery & Registration

NTSA registration, delivery to your location

Document Checklist
  • Original Bill of Lading (B/L)
    Issued by shipping line
  • Commercial Invoice
    Vehicle value, make, model, specifications
  • Packing List
    Vehicle details and any accessories
  • Original Logbook/Title
    Vehicle ownership from country of origin
  • QISJ Inspection Certificate
    Mandatory pre-shipment inspection
  • KRA Import Declaration Form (IDF)
    Kenya Tradex arranges this
  • Your KRA PIN Number
    Required for IDF application
  • Passport/ID Copy
    For identity verification
Note: Export certificate of origin is NOT required for Kenya imports.

Cost-Saving Tip: Always obtain QISJ inspection before shipping. Without it, KEBS charges a 5% local inspection surcharge that can add thousands to your import costs.
Shipping Methods

Roll-on/Roll-off (RoRo)

Cost: USD 1,500 - 3,500
Best for: Standard vehicles
Pros: Most economical
Cons: Vehicle exposed during voyage

Container Shipping

Cost: USD 2,500 - 5,500
Best for: High-value vehicles
Pros: Better protection
Cons: More expensive

NTSA Registration Checklist
  • Vehicle inspection at NTSA center
  • Submit customs release documents
  • Pay NTSA registration fees
  • Obtain registration certificate
  • Apply for number plates (2-4 weeks)
  • Obtain new logbook
Returning Residents: If you've lived abroad for 12+ months, you may qualify for duty-free import. Requirements: owned vehicle abroad 12+ months, returned to Kenya within 3 months, and spent less than 90 days in Kenya during the 12-month abroad period.
