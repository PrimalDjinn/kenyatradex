Mombasa Port Clearance Checklist

Complete guide to clearing goods at the Port of Mombasa, Kenya

Phase 1: Pre-Shipment Documents
  • Import Declaration Form (IDF)
    Obtain from KRA via iCMS before shipment arrives. Required for all imports by sea or air.
  • Bill of Lading (B/L)
    Original B/L or Sea Waybill from shipping line.
  • Commercial Invoice
    Must show transaction value, currency, incoterms, and item description.
  • Packing List
    Detailed breakdown of contents, quantities, weights, packaging type.
  • Certificate of Origin
    Required for EAC preferential duty rates. Issued by exporting country's chamber of commerce.
  • Any Other Relevant Document
    As required by specific cargo type or regulatory agencies.
Phase 2: Regulatory Permits (If Applicable)
NEMA National Environment Management Authority - Extended Producer Responsibility

KEPHIS Kenya Plant Health Inspectorate Service - Agricultural products
VMD Veterinary Medicines Directorate - Animal products & medicines
KEBS Kenya Bureau of Standards - Quality & standards compliance
Phase 3: IDF Registration Process
  • Register IDF (IMO) in iCMS
    Create official cargo record in KRA system using IM0 regime.
  • Pay UCR Fee
    Pay Unique Consignment Reference fee: USD 10. Links cargo to all OGAs.
  • Obtain Required Permits
    Submit permits to iCMS based on cargo type from relevant agencies.
  • Await IDF Approval
    IDF approved after permits and fees confirmed. Await vessel arrival and manifest.
  • Register Customs Entry
    Register entry in iCMS using your PIN. Receive email confirmation.
  • Generate E-Slip & Pay Taxes
    Generate e-slip, pay taxes at KRA-appointed bank, keep receipt.
Phase 4: Port Clearance Process
  • Vessel Arrival & Docking
    Container assigned CY location or loaded to CFS for deconsolidation. Receive Arrival Notice.
  • Document Submission
    Submit all documents via iCMS. KRA assesses and calculates duties based on HS code.
  • Duty Payment
    Pay duties via bank or iCMS e-payment. Pay KPA charges separately.
  • Physical Inspection (If Selected)
    KRA may select for x-ray or physical inspection. Adds 2-5 working days.
  • Release Order
    KRA issues release order. Receive email confirmation. Agent processes Delivery Order.
  • Obtain Delivery Order
    Collect Delivery Order from shipping line agent upon release confirmation.
  • Container Pickup
    With release order & DO, enter port, collect container. Port exit: 2-4 hours.
Expected Clearance Timeline
Scenario Timeline
Complete documentation, no inspection 24-48 hours
Minor document errors, quick correction 3-5 working days
Physical inspection required 2-5 working days
Restricted goods, additional permits 1-3 weeks
