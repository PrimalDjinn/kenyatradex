Major Chinese Ports for Kenya Shipping

  • Shanghai (SHA): China's largest port, most frequent sailings to Mombasa (weekly departures). Best for general cargo and electronics.
  • Ningbo (NGB): Major hub for machinery, industrial equipment, and auto parts. Competitive rates.
  • Guangzhou (Huangpu/Nansha): Ideal for textiles, garments, furniture, and consumer goods from the Pearl River Delta.
  • Shenzhen (Yantian): Best for electronics, high-tech products, and e-commerce cargo. Fast loading times.
  • Tianjin (TSN): Gateway to Beijing and northern China. Good for automotive parts, steel, and heavy machinery.
Need help calculating cargo volume? CBM (Cubic Meters) = Length × Width × Height (in meters). Example: A pallet measuring 1.2m × 1.0m × 1.5m = 1.8 CBM. Contact us for accurate volume estimation.

Documents Required for Import from China to Kenya

  • Bill of Lading (Sea) / Airway Bill (Air): Proof of shipment issued by carrier
  • Commercial Invoice: Detailed list of goods with values and HS codes
  • Packing List: Itemized list of cargo with weights and dimensions
  • Import Declaration Form (IDF): Obtained from KRA before cargo arrival
  • KEBS Certificate of Conformity (CoC): Required for regulated products (electronics, toys, food contact materials)

Frequently Asked Questions - China to Kenya Shipping

  • How long does shipping from China to Kenya take?
    Ocean freight from China to Mombasa takes 20-30 days depending on the port of origin. Air freight takes 3-5 days. Kenya Tradex handles the entire process from pickup in China to delivery in Kenya.
  • What is the cheapest way to ship from China to Kenya?
    Sea freight (ocean freight) is the cheapest way to ship from China to Kenya. A 20ft container typically costs USD 1,500-3,000, while air freight costs significantly more (USD 5-8 per kg). For smaller shipments under 15 CBM, LCL (shared container) is most cost-effective.
  • How much does a 20ft container cost from China to Kenya?
    Shipping a 20ft container from China to Kenya typically costs between USD 1,500 and USD 3,000 depending on the port of origin (Shanghai, Ningbo, Guangzhou, Shenzhen), current market rates, and seasonal demand. Contact Kenya Tradex for real-time pricing.
  • What are the major Chinese ports for shipping to Kenya?
    Major ports include Shanghai, Ningbo, Guangzhou (Huangpu/Nansha), Shenzhen (Yantian), and Tianjin. Shipping from Shanghai and Ningbo typically offers more frequent sailings and competitive rates.
  • What documents are required to import from China to Kenya?
    Required documents include: Bill of Lading (sea) or Airway Bill (air), Commercial Invoice, Packing List, KRA Import Declaration Form (IDF), and KEBS Certificate of Conformity (CoC) for regulated products. Kenya Tradex handles all documentation.
