Warehousing locations and use cases

Storage needs can vary depending on whether the cargo is linked to port activity, inland distribution, project logistics or multi-country delivery planning. Kenya Tradex supports warehousing needs linked to both Mombasa and Nairobi operations.

  • Mombasa Warehouse: Minutes from Mombasa port — ideal for rapid cargo transfer, customs clearance, and transit bonding to Uganda, Rwanda, DRC, and South Sudan.
  • Nairobi Warehouse: Strategic distribution hub for Kenya and regional exports — perfect for inventory serving local and East African markets.
  • Importers & Exporters – Temporary storage before customs clearance
  • Manufacturers – Raw material and finished goods storage
  • Distributors & Wholesalers – Regional inventory hubs
  • E-commerce Businesses – Fulfillment and last-mile distribution
  • Project Cargo Clients – Temporary storage for equipment and machinery

Warehousing questions

  • What warehousing services does Kenya Tradex provide?
    Kenya Tradex supports bonded storage, private warehousing, 24/7 security, inventory management, pick & pack services, cross-docking and onward distribution planning in Mombasa and Nairobi.
  • What is bonded warehousing?
    Bonded warehousing allows imported cargo to be stored under customs control before duty is settled for local release, subject to applicable customs rules and storage conditions.
  • Do you support short-term and longer-term storage?
    Yes. Kenya Tradex supports storage planning for short-term (daily/weekly) and long-term (monthly/yearly) warehousing needs depending on cargo type and handling requirements.
  • Is your warehouse secure with 24/7 monitoring?
    Yes. Kenya Tradex facilities feature 24/7 security and CCTV monitoring to ensure your cargo is protected at all times.
