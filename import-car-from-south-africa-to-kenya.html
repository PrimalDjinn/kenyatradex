What to check before shipping from South Africa

  • Vehicle suitability: Confirm the unit is appropriate for Kenya use and that the file can support clearance once it lands.
  • Documentation readiness: Ensure seller documents, shipping paperwork, and importer details are available before departure.
  • Inspection planning: Review inspection and compliance requirements early to avoid delays after arrival.
  • Arrival timing: Short sea transit does not remove the need for pre-arrival planning, especially for customs and delivery coordination.
  • Delivery destination: Decide early whether the car should remain in Mombasa, move to Nairobi, or continue to another destination after clearance.
  • Model condition checks: Buyers should still review service history, accident history, and mechanical condition before committing.

South Africa car import questions

  • Can I import a car from South Africa to Kenya?
    Yes. South Africa is a practical sourcing market for Kenya because of route speed, right-hand-drive vehicle availability, and strong supply of SUVs, pickups, and commercial vehicles.
  • How long does South Africa to Kenya shipping take?
    Many shipments on this route are planned around roughly 7 to 14 sea transit days, then customs clearance and onward delivery after vessel arrival at Mombasa.
  • What type of vehicles are commonly imported from South Africa?
    Importers often look at SUVs, pickups, vans, and commercial units, especially when they want vehicles suited to regional roads and business use.
  • Should I calculate import cost before booking shipment?
    Yes. It is best to estimate total landed cost before shipment so you can compare the vehicle price, freight, clearance, and delivery cost together.
  • Can Kenya Tradex clear the vehicle after arrival?
    Yes. Kenya Tradex supports customs clearance at Mombasa and coordinates the move to Nairobi or another agreed destination after release.
