Clearing cargo through Mombasa starts before the vessel arrives. Importers should align the commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, importer details, permits and any agency requirements early.

## Key documents

- Bill of lading or telex release details.
- Commercial invoice and packing list.
- Import Declaration Form where required.
- KEBS, PVOC, permits or agency approvals for regulated cargo.
- Consignee details and final delivery destination.

## Practical clearance flow

1. Review the cargo file before arrival.
2. Confirm HS code, valuation, taxes and agency exposure.
3. Lodge the customs entry and respond to any queries.
4. Pay applicable charges and coordinate port release.
5. Arrange delivery or transit movement.
