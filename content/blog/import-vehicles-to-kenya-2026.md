Vehicle imports into Kenya need careful checks before purchase and shipment. Age limits, CRSP value, documents and inspection requirements can affect whether the vehicle clears smoothly.

## Typical documents

- Export certificate or logbook.
- Commercial invoice.
- Bill of lading.
- Inspection certificate where applicable.
- Importer PIN and consignee details.

Kenya Tradex helps importers review vehicle details, estimate duty exposure and plan clearance in Kenya.
