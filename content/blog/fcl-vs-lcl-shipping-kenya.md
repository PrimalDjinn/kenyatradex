FCL and LCL serve different cargo profiles. The best choice depends on volume, urgency, sensitivity, supplier readiness and destination handling.

## When FCL fits

- Larger cargo volumes.
- Sensitive cargo that should not share space.
- Faster destination handling.
- Shipments where dedicated container control matters.

## When LCL fits

- Smaller shipments under a full container load.
- Samples, trial orders and mixed supplier cargo.
- Importers testing a new product lane.

Kenya Tradex helps importers compare FCL, LCL, customs clearance and delivery options through Mombasa.
