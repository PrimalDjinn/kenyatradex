A transit bond allows cargo to move through Kenya toward a final destination in another country without paying Kenyan import duties as local consumption cargo.

## When transit planning matters

- Cargo moving from Mombasa to Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, DRC or South Sudan.
- Shipments requiring border coordination.
- Files where consignee, route and final destination must align.

Kenya Tradex supports transit bond coordination and regional delivery planning for landlocked markets.
