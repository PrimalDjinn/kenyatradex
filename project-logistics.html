Project logistics questions

  • What is project logistics?
    Project logistics involves planning and coordinating the movement of heavy, oversized or operationally complex cargo that requires special handling, route review, timing control and delivery planning for large-scale developments like mining, energy and infrastructure projects.
  • What types of cargo can Kenya Tradex support under project logistics?
    Kenya Tradex supports project logistics for heavy equipment (20-200+ tons), oversized machinery, mining crushers and excavators, wind turbine components, oil and gas infrastructure, transformers, generators, industrial presses, and complete plant relocation.
  • Do you support project cargo moving beyond Kenya?
    Yes. Kenya Tradex supports project cargo planning for regional delivery into Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, the Democratic Republic of the Congo, South Sudan, and Tanzania — including major project sites like Turkana (oil fields), Hoima (Uganda oil), Lake Kivu (methane gas), and Lubumbashi (copper/cobalt mining).
