Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about freight forwarding and customs clearance

What freight forwarding services does Kenya Tradex provide?

Kenya Tradex provides comprehensive freight forwarding services including ocean freight (FCL/LCL), air freight, customs clearance, warehousing, overland transport, project logistics, vehicle import, and shipping from China. We operate from Mombasa and Nairobi serving Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, DRC, South Sudan, and Tanzania.

How long does customs clearance take at Mombasa port?

Customs clearance at Mombasa port typically takes 24-72 hours depending on cargo type, documentation completeness, and inspection requirements. Kenya Tradex works to expedite this process through proper documentation and KRA compliance.

Does Kenya Tradex handle transit cargo to Uganda, Rwanda, and other landlocked countries?

Yes, Kenya Tradex handles transit cargo to Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, DRC, South Sudan, and Tanzania. We provide transit bonds, coordinate with relevant customs authorities, and manage documentation for seamless cross-border cargo movement through the Northern and Central corridors.

Does Kenya Tradex have warehouses in Nairobi and Mombasa?

Yes, Kenya Tradex has warehousing facilities in both Mombasa and Nairobi with bonded storage options. Our warehouses offer 24/7 security, inventory management, pick and pack services, cross-docking, and distribution planning for cargo destined across East and Central Africa.

How can I get a freight quote from Kenya Tradex?

You can request a freight quote by calling +254 721 596 259, messaging via WhatsApp, or filling out the quote form on our website. Provide your cargo details including type, weight, dimensions, origin, destination, and timing. We respond to quote requests with urgency.
