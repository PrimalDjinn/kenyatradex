LCL Consolidation: Cost-effective shipping for smaller cargo

If your cargo volume is under 15 cubic meters, Less than Container Load (LCL) consolidation allows you to share container space with other shipments. You pay only for the space you use — ideal for samples, smaller orders, or testing new markets.

When LCL consolidation makes sense

  • Small Businesses & Startups: Test new markets without full container commitment.
  • Sample or trial orders: Useful for businesses testing products or new markets.
  • Multi-supplier cargo: Practical when goods from different suppliers need to move in one shared freight flow.
  • Cost-sensitive shipments: Helps avoid full-container costs for lighter or lower-volume cargo.
  • E-commerce fulfillment: Regular smaller shipments to international customers.

How LCL consolidation works

  • Step 1: Share your cargo details, dimensions, weight and destination.
  • Step 2: Shipments are grouped into shared container space based on route.
  • Step 3: Documentation and customs processing are coordinated.
  • Step 4: Cargo is loaded and moved through the sea freight route.
  • Step 5: Cargo is deconsolidated and prepared for onward delivery.

Ocean freight questions

  • What ocean freight services does Kenya Tradex provide?
    Kenya Tradex supports full container load (FCL), less than container load (LCL) consolidation, customs clearance, transit bonds, and inland delivery coordination for cargo moving through Mombasa port.
  • Do you handle cargo moving beyond Kenya to landlocked countries?
    Yes. Kenya Tradex supports cargo moving from Mombasa into Uganda, Rwanda, Burundi, the Democratic Republic of the Congo and South Sudan using transit bonds for seamless customs clearance at borders.
  • Which shipping lines do you partner with?
    We partner with major shipping lines including Maersk, PIL, MSC, CMA CGM, COSCO, and Hapag-Lloyd to offer competitive rates and reliable schedules from Mombasa port.
  • Do you offer real-time tracking for ocean freight shipments?
    Yes. Kenya Tradex provides real-time container tracking with end-to-end visibility from port of origin to final delivery destination.
  • What is LCL consolidation and when should I use it?
    LCL (Less than Container Load) consolidation combines smaller shipments into shared container space. It's ideal when your cargo volume is under 15 cubic meters and doesn't justify a full container. You pay only for the space you use — cost-effective for samples, smaller orders, and testing new markets.
  • What's the difference between FCL and LCL?
    FCL (Full Container Load) gives you exclusive use of a 20ft or 40ft container — faster and safer for large shipments. LCL shares container space with other cargo — more economical for shipments under 15 CBM. Kenya Tradex offers both options from Mombasa port.
