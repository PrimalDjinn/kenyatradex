Regional transit cargo support

Transit cargo and road freight from Mombasa to Juba, Kigali, Kampala, DRC and South Sudan.

Kenya Tradex helps importers and traders plan cargo movement from the Port of Mombasa into landlocked East and Central African markets with transit bond coordination, customs documentation and road freight planning.

Transit routes supported

Mombasa to Kampala

Road freight and transit cargo planning for Kampala, Jinja and inland Uganda routes through the Northern Corridor, with border and delivery coordination.

Mombasa to Kigali

Transit cargo planning for Kigali and Bujumbura using suitable corridor and border arrangements for the shipment profile.

Mombasa to Juba and DRC

Selected cargo movement to Juba, Goma, Bukavu and related destinations where route, document and timing control matter.

What transit cargo usually requires

  • Correct consignee, destination and customs documentation before release from Mombasa.
  • Transit bond coordination and matching the route to the final country requirements.
  • Road manifest, border planning and communication between clearing, transport and receiving teams.
  • Delivery planning around cargo type, value, urgency, weight and final destination access.
