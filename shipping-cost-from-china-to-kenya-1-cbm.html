What usually makes 1 CBM cost go up

  • Origin city and port: Shanghai, Ningbo, Guangzhou, and Shenzhen do not always price or sail the same way.
  • Origin handling: Local handling, export paperwork, and warehouse charges can materially change a 1 CBM quote.
  • Destination handling: Small shipments can feel expensive because destination-side LCL charges are spread over a small cargo volume.
  • Customs and delivery: Clearance scope and inland delivery distance should always be included in your budgeting.
  • Cargo type: Fragile, regulated, or awkwardly packed cargo can attract extra handling or compliance costs.
Need help calculating volume? CBM (Cubic Meters) = Length × Width × Height in meters. If your shipment is close to 1 CBM, small measurement errors can distort the quote, so it is worth confirming the actual packing dimensions. Ask us for a volume check.

When 1 CBM LCL is a good idea

  • Test orders: Good for importers validating a product before committing to larger volumes.
  • Low-volume replenishment: Useful when you need stock but do not want to wait until you can fill a container.
  • Mixed cartons: Small retail cargo or bundled supplier purchases can fit well into an LCL model.
  • Market testing: Suitable for SMEs and traders who want to limit exposure while still importing from China.
  • Not always the cheapest total option: If volume grows, FCL or a different consolidation strategy may become better value.

1 CBM shipping cost questions

  • How much is 1 CBM from China to Kenya?
    The answer depends on more than the ocean rate. You need freight, origin handling, destination handling, customs clearance, and delivery in one estimate.
  • Is 1 CBM always best as LCL?
    Usually yes, but not always. Very urgent cargo may fit air freight better, while growing volume may eventually justify FCL.
  • How long will it take?
    Expect the standard China sea-freight window plus extra time for LCL consolidation and destination handling.

    That is fine. Kenya Tradex can estimate the volume from the packing dimensions and help you avoid underestimating the shipment.
