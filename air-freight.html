Moi International Airport cargo support in Mombasa

For cargo moving through Moi International Airport (MBA), Kenya Tradex supports importers and exporters who need practical cargo clearance, air freight documentation and onward delivery planning. Kenya Tradex is not the airport operator, but we can help with the freight and customs steps around a commercial shipment.

  • Airport cargo guidance: We help clients understand what cargo details, Airway Bill information and consignee documents are needed before asking for release support.
  • Mombasa air freight support: Useful for urgent imports, samples, spare parts, perishables and commercial shipments entering through MBA.
  • Transparent contact intent: If you need Kenya Tradex cargo clearance help, use the quote form or WhatsApp link on this page. If you need the airport authority directly, confirm the official contact through the airport operator.
