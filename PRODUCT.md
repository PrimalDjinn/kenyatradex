# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Importers, exporters, manufacturers, traders, vehicle importers, and project cargo teams moving goods through Kenya and East African trade corridors. They need clear logistics guidance, fast quotation paths, compliance confidence, and direct contact with a licensed freight partner in Mombasa and Nairobi.

## Product Purpose

Kenya Tradex is a marketing and lead-generation website for freight forwarding, customs clearance, warehousing, overland transport, project logistics, vehicle import, and cross-border trade services. Success means preserving search visibility, communicating operational credibility, and converting visitors into phone, WhatsApp, email, or quote-form inquiries without changing existing service behavior.

## Brand Personality

Licensed, direct, dependable. The site should feel like an experienced logistics operator: practical, authoritative, responsive, and regionally grounded rather than decorative or abstract.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page patterns, vague corporate logistics imagery, fragile luxury styling, playful startup language, and visual treatments that make the company feel unlicensed or experimental. Do not hide operational details, credentials, service areas, or quote paths behind overly clever layouts.

## Design Principles

Lead with operational trust: surface licenses, locations, service coverage, and direct contact paths clearly.

Preserve SEO intent: keep page topics, metadata, structured content, and internal linking recognizable through the Nuxt rebuild.

Make quotation fast: every service page should keep a low-friction path to request a quote or contact the team.

Use confidence over decoration: improve hierarchy, rhythm, and responsiveness while retaining the navy/red Kenya Tradex identity.

Design for cargo decisions: explain routes, compliance steps, documents, and timing in plain language for busy commercial users.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target accessible, responsive pages with keyboard-friendly navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, form labels or accessible names, and reduced-motion-safe interactions. Maintain mobile usability for users contacting the business from phones in the field.
