Air cargo clearance priorities

Speed

Air cargo is usually chosen because time matters. We help check the customs file early so preventable documentation issues do not consume the time saved by air freight.

Correct documents

Airway Bill, invoice, packing list, permits and importer details should align with the shipment and customs declaration.

Controlled cargo

Pharma, perishables, electronics, parts and other regulated goods may require additional handling, inspection or agency coordination.

Common JKIA cargo types

  • Urgent spare parts and manufacturing inputs.
  • Medical, pharma and temperature-sensitive cargo.
  • Samples, ecommerce, documents and courier-linked shipments.
  • Perishables, high-value items and time-bound business cargo.
