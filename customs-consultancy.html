EAC Common External Tariff (CET) Duty Rates

The East African Community applies a common tariff structure for imports from outside the EAC. Below are the standard duty rates by product category:

CategoryDuty RateExamples
Raw Materials0%Unprocessed agricultural products, minerals, crude oil
Semi-processed Goods10%Basic chemicals, processed agricultural products
Intermediate Goods10% - 25%Components, parts, industrial supplies
Finished Consumer Goods25% - 35%Electronics, household items, clothing
EAC-origin Goods0% (with valid Certificate of Origin)Goods manufactured within EAC partner states

Additional charges: VAT (16%), IDF fee (3.5% of CIF), and RDL (2% of CIF). Contact us for product-specific duty rates.

Common Customs Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

  • Incorrect HS Code Classification – Can lead to overpayment (if higher duty rate) or penalties (if underpaid). Solution: Engage customs experts to verify classifications before shipment.
  • Missing EAC Certificate of Origin – Paying 25-35% duty instead of 0% on EAC-origin goods. Solution: Obtain valid Certificates of Origin from authorized bodies.
  • Incomplete Commercial Invoices – Leads to clearance delays and potential penalties. Solution: Ensure invoices include full product descriptions, unit values, and total CIF value.
  • Not Claiming Duty Drawback – Leaving money on the table when re-exporting goods. Solution: Apply for duty drawback within 12 months of export.
  • Poor Documentation for Post-Clearance Audits – Risk of penalties if records are incomplete. Solution: Maintain organized import/export records for at least 5 years.

Customs consultancy questions

  • What is customs consultancy and why do I need it?
    Customs consultancy helps businesses navigate complex import/export regulations, minimize duties legally, avoid penalties, and ensure compliance with KRA and EAC customs laws. Kenya Tradex provides expert advisory to reduce costs and clearance delays.
  • How can I reduce import duties legally in Kenya?
    You can reduce import duties through proper tariff classification, utilizing EAC rules of origin for preferential rates, applying for duty drawback on re-exports, and securing VAT suspensions or exemptions. Kenya Tradex guides you through all these legal mechanisms.
  • What is a post-clearance audit and how can you help?
    A post-clearance audit is when KRA reviews past import declarations for compliance. Kenya Tradex helps you prepare documentation, respond to audit queries, correct errors, and minimize potential penalties or additional duty assessments.
  • What documents are required for customs clearance in Kenya?
    Key documents include Import Declaration Form (IDF), Bill of Lading or Airway Bill, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Certificate of Origin (for EAC preferential rates), and Form C. Kenya Tradex handles all documentation for seamless clearance.
  • How much does customs consultancy cost in Kenya?
    Customs consultancy fees vary based on the scope of work. Kenya Tradex offers flexible engagement models: hourly advisory, project-based fixed fees, or retainer agreements for ongoing compliance. Contact us for a tailored quote based on your specific needs.
  • How long does customs clearance take at Mombasa port?
    Customs clearance at Mombasa port typically takes 24-72 hours depending on cargo type, documentation completeness, and inspection requirements. Our consultancy helps ensure documentation is correct upfront to avoid delays.
